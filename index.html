How it works

Three steps, all inside your browser tab.

1

Load the model

A ~2 MB TensorFlow.js model is downloaded once and cached by the browser.

2

Prepare the image

Your image is resized to 224×224 pixels, the input size the network was trained on.

3

Predict

The network outputs a probability for each class: blurry or clear. The highest one wins.
